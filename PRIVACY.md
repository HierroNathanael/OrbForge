# Orbforge — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

Orbforge ("the bot") is a Discord bot for playing a text-based RPG. This
policy explains what data the bot stores and how to request its removal.

## What we store

When you use Orbforge, we store:

- **Your Discord user ID** — used to link your account to your character(s).
  We do not store your Discord username, avatar, email, or any other
  Discord profile information.
- **Character and gameplay data** you create or generate by playing —
  character name, class, level, experience, stats, inventory items,
  in-game currency (Gold and Orbs), skill tree/skill progress, and trade
  history with other players.

We do **not** collect payment information, IP addresses, device
information, or message content (the bot only reads slash-command
interactions, not your messages).

## How we use it

Stored data is used solely to run the game: tracking your character's
progress, inventory, and in-game economy across sessions. We do not sell,
share, or use this data for advertising or any purpose outside operating
the bot.

## Data retention & deletion

Data is retained for as long as your character(s) exist. To request
deletion of your data (your Discord ID and all associated characters and
gameplay data), contact us at the email below — we will delete it within
a reasonable time.

## Where data is stored

Gameplay data is stored in a MongoDB database operated by the bot's
maintainer. It is not shared with third parties.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here with a new
"Last updated" date.
